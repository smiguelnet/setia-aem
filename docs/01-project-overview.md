# 01 - Project Overview

## Project Information

**Project Name:** Setia Corporate Website  
**Platform:** Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) as a Cloud Service  
**Project Type:** Institutional Corporate Website  
**Version:** 1.0.0-SNAPSHOT

## Business Objectives

The Setia website is an institutional corporate website designed to:

- Showcase company services and capabilities
- Present technology solutions and platforms
- Generate leads through engagement
- Provide contact and call-to-action touchpoints
- Build brand presence and credibility
- Highlight partnerships and expertise

## Target Audience

**Primary:**
- Enterprise decision-makers
- Technology leaders
- Business stakeholders seeking software outsourcing partners
- Companies looking for digital transformation solutions

**Secondary:**
- Potential partners
- Technology vendors
- Industry analysts
- Recruitment candidates

## Website Scope

### Pages
1. **Home** - Hero banner with service highlights and engagement sections
2. **Company** - Company presentation, story, downloads, and partner showcase
3. **Services** - Detailed service offerings with interactive components
4. **Solutions** - Technology platform and feature demonstrations

### Key Features
- Fixed navigation header
- Responsive design
- Interactive components (accordions, galleries, cards)
- Downloadable resources
- Call-to-action sections
- Partner logo showcase

## Project Team Roles

### Developers
Focus on:
- Component development (`ui.apps`)
- Backend logic (`core`)
- Frontend assets (`ui.frontend`)
- Build and deployment

### Content Authors
Focus on:
- Page creation and editing (AEM Author UI)
- Content management
- Asset management (DAM)
- Visual composition

### Administrators
Focus on:
- Template and policy configuration
- Experience Fragment management
- User permissions
- System configuration

## Constraints and Assumptions

### Constraints
- Single language/country site (initially)
- No e-commerce functionality
- No user authentication/login
- No dynamic content personalization (initially)

### Assumptions
- Content will be provided by marketing team
- Images and assets will be provided in web-optimized formats
- Brand guidelines and design system exist
- AEM environment is provisioned and accessible
